# Portfolio presentation

- The public name is Douglas C Martins in the hero, identification, footer, and share title. DCMARTINS.CO stays the brand in the footer.
- The page opens in Brazilian Portuguese. A header control switches PT-BR, EN, and ES without a reload.
- The header is a full-width sticky bar: `<dcmartins />` text mark, section links, LinkedIn, Gmail, GitHub, and the language switch. There is no logo image.
- The hero order is availability, name, role, summary, a single row of real technologies, then GitHub, CV download, and LinkedIn buttons. The circular portrait sits on the right and keeps the head and shoulders in frame.
- The whole site is dark (Lovable-inspired terminal look, portfolio colors). particles.js runs on a fixed canvas behind the content and stays off when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- Experience is a grid of cards; "Read more" opens the prerendered `/experience/[id]` page. Never expand detail inline.
- Experience leads with the problem and the outcome. Greenn shows measured results before the technical detail. The other roles use short narratives. Do not reduce a job to a list of acronyms.
- Testimonials use the written recommendations and the photos in `public/images/depoimentos/`. Quotes stay in the original Portuguese in every language.
- Contact is a mailto button plus email, phone, GitHub, and LinkedIn. There is no message form.
- The CV download serves `/cv/Douglas_da_Cruz_Martins.pdf` (editable source in `cv/`, mirrored in `public/cv/`). Replace only when asked; do not publish unless asked.
